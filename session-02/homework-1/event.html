City Tech Conference 2026: Building a Better Web

📅 Date & Time: Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 9:00 AM

📍 Location: Metro Convention Center, Hall B

Join developers, designers and engineers for a full day of talks and hands-on learning. You will explore the future of the open web, practice building accessible interfaces, and discuss how AI is changing the way we write software.

Featured Schedule Highlights

  • 09:30 AM — Keynote: The Future of the Open Web, with Dr. Maya Collins
  • 11:00 AM — Workshop: Hands-on Accessibility, with Daniel Ortiz
  • 02:00 PM — Panel: Engineering in the AI Era, with Priya Nair and Lucas Meyer

Speakers

Maya Collins, Daniel Ortiz, Priya Nair and Lucas Meyer.

Venue

Metro Convention Center, Hall B. Step-free access and an on-site quiet room are available.
